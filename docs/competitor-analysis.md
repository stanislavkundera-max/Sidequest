---

## The competitor

- **SideQuest: Nearby Activities** — 10K+ Play downloads, 4.6★ (129 reviews), Travel & Local.
  About 19 ratings on iOS → Android-first, probably India-heavy (Standa's hypothesis).
- One developer (Adhish Paranjothi, "SideQuests HQ"), built with Lovable, Claude and Cloudflare.
  Claims 8,000+ organic downloads in the first month.
- Keeps people with daily mini quests, streaks, a daily push, a feed of others ("Lobby"), group
  games (Rooms) and friend tagging.
- *Fyn: Daily Challenges & Habits* — same idea with a paid tier, only 100+ downloads.

## How it grew

- **Seven renames** toward what people type: "Log Your Lore" → … → "Things To Do" → "Nearby
  Activities". Category moved Lifestyle → Travel & Local.
- **SEO-style description** repeating *things to do, near you, hidden gems, explore your city*.
- **Reddit build-in-public** in r/SidequestsIRL, asking for feedback.
- No TikTok or Instagram, no press, no visible ads.

## Where we stand

- Title is the brand alone (15 of 30 characters), category Lifestyle.
- Short description sells a feeling; "side quests" is its only search phrase.
- What we have and it doesn't: memories at the core (photo + feeling, the cairn), a calm tone with
  no streaks, a name that isn't just "SideQuest" among 10+ apps of that name.

## Not doing — and why

So the same ideas from the growth playbook aren't reopened.

| Idea | Why not |
|---|---|
| Streaks, streak freeze, levels | Decided against 2026-08-06; AGENTS.md |
| One push a day | "No endless notifications"; #68's weekly rhythm answers the same need |
| Feed of others, featured community photos | Social feed and UGC — AGENTS.md, Google's UGC policy |
| Rooms, friend tagging inside the app | Multiplayer and in-app social; the off-app invite is #55b |
| "Stop scrolling / scroll less" headline | Anti-screen-time framing, ruled out by the mentor |
| Keyword-stuffed description | Mentor wants feeling-first; Play's metadata policy forbids it |
| Renaming the brand | Test a descriptor after the name instead; the brand stays |
| Travel & Local now | Promises places near you, which the app doesn't do before #54 |
